The [Pressa in Cologne in 1928](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pressa) has been a topic in this blog before, as some relevant buildings were erected in preparation for it. Here is a publication that appeared as part of the exhibition and presents various German newspapers including their buildings. Not everything is Brick Expressionism.

On the back of the publication there is a type specimen, which can be found on ["Schriftmuster"](https://schriftmuster.projektemacher.org/post/fette-metropolis) (German).
