This book belongs to the series of "Blauen Bücher"...
<!--more-->
More precisely, to books published in this series by [Walter Müller-Wulckow](https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Walter_M%C3%BCller-Wulckow) (German), which presented the then-modern architecture and interior design. This is the last of the three; the others can be found on the overview page on [Walter Müller-Wulckow](/tags/Walter-Müller-Wulckow/).
This volume, as the title already reveals, is about community buildings such as civic halls and churches. Buildings by the following architects are illustrated:

* [Johannes Göderitz](https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johannes_G%C3%B6deritz) (German)
* [Friedrich Fischer](https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Friedrich_Fischer_(Architekt)) (German)
* [Wilhelm Wagner](https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wilhelm_Wagner_(Architekt)) (German)
* [Hermann Höger](https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hermann_H%C3%B6ger) (German)
* [Fritz Schumacher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fritz_Schumacher)
* [Wilhelm Kreis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wilhelm_Kreis)
* [Alfred Fischer](https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alfred_Fischer_(Architekt)) (German)
* [Hans Poelzig](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hans_Poelzig)
