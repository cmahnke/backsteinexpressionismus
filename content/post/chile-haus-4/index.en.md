A view of the [Chilehaus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chilehaus) and [Ballinhaus (today Messberghof)](https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Me%C3%9Fberghof) (German) in Hamburg.

<!--more-->

Largest office building in Hamburg
with approx. 35000 sqm. "Triolin" cast floor covering by
Köln-Rottweil Aktiengesellschaft, Berlin, installed.

"TRIOLIN" was awarded
Gold Medals at the
Industrial Exhibition for the
Building Trade, Housing and
related trades in Nuremberg
April-May 1924, at the
Exhibition for Culture and Economy
in Aussig (Czech. Slovakia) 1924.
The Building Exhibition
Stuttgart 1924 brought Triolin the
Württemberg STATE MEDAL in silver.
