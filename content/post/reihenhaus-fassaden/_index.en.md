This book is a true treasure trove, not only for Brick Expressionism...

Therefore, interesting designs in other styles and other buildings by well-known architects are also part of this contribution. The author, [Werner Hegemann](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Werner_Hegemann), was an urban planner and architecture critic and, at the same publishing house, also editor of the journals ["Wasmuths Monatshefte für Baukunst"](https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wasmuths_Monatshefte_f%C3%BCr_Baukunst) (German) and ["Städtebau"](https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Der_St%C3%A4dtebau) (German), which themselves could also be good sources for this blog.
