This book from 1938 contains many buildings in the style of the "3rd Reich", such as barracks, ministries, Ordensburgen and also autobahns. But it also includes some industrial buildings that can be assigned to the "Neues Bauen" movement, and a building by [Fritz Höger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fritz_H%C3%B6ger), the Rüstringen town hall.
