This book belongs to the series of "Blauen Bücher"...
<!--more-->
More precisely, to books published in this series by [Walter Müller-Wulckow](https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Walter_M%C3%BCller-Wulckow) (German), which presented the then-modern architecture and interior design. More volumes from this series will be presented in the future.
This volume, as the title already reveals, is about commercial buildings. Buildings by the following architects are illustrated:
* [Fritz Schumacher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fritz_Schumacher)
* [Alfred Fischer](https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alfred_Fischer_(Architekt)) (German)
* [Fritz Höger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fritz_H%C3%B6ger)
* [Hans and Oskar Gerson](https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hans_und_Oskar_Gerson) (German)
* [Edmund Körner](https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edmund_K%C3%B6rner) (German)
* [Hanns Hopp](https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hanns_Hopp) (German)
* [Wilhelm Kreis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wilhelm_Kreis)
* [Jacob Koerfer](https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacob_Koerfer) (German)
* [Eugen Schmohl](https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eugen_Schmohl) (German)
* [Hans Poelzig](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hans_Poelzig)
* [Peter Behrens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_Behrens)
* [Paul Bonatz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paul_Bonatz)

Not everything shown can be counted as Brick Expressionism...
