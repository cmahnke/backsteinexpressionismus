This book belongs to the series of "Blue Books"...
<!--more-->
More precisely, to a book published in this series by [Walter Müller-Wulckow](https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Walter_M%C3%BCller-Wulckow) (German), which presented the then-modern architecture and interior design. Another volume from this series will be presented in the future.
In this volume, as the title already reveals, the focus is on residential buildings. Buildings by the following architects are illustrated:
* [Otto Bartning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Otto_Bartning)
* [Bernhard Hoetger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bernhard_Hoetger)
