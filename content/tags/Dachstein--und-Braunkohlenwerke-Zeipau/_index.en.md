# The Dachstein- und Braunkohlenwerke Zeipau in...
* [International Architecture Database](https://deu.archinform.net/projekte/4016.htm) (German)
* [International Database and Gallery of Structures (Structurae)](https://structurae.net/en/bauwerke/wasserturm-zeipau)

The director's house is now destroyed; the water tower still exists today in Jankowa Żagańska, Szczepanów, municipality of [Iłowa](https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/I%C5%82owa) (German), Poland (formerly near Zeipau, Silesia). Current images can be found on a [page about Polish water towers](https://wiezecisnien.eu/lubuskie/jankowa-zaganska_cegielnia/) (German) – which is worth a visit even if you don't understand the text...
