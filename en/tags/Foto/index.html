Die deutsche Zeitung - Ihr Werden, Wesen und Wirken
The Pressa in Cologne in 1928 has been a topic in this blog before, as some relevant buildings were erected in preparation for it. Here is a publication that appeared as part of the exhibition and presents various German newspapers including their buildings. Not everything is Brick Expressionism. On the back of the publication there is a type specimen, which can be found on “Schriftmuster” (German).
